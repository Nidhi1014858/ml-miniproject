How the Model Works

Trained on simulated satellite telemetry records using scikit-learn's Decision Tree Classifier.

ℹ️

Realistic Data & Expected Accuracy

During data generation, exactly 6% of packet labels were randomly shifted by one step to imitate human operator discretion, queue preemption, and real-world transmission noise. Because of this intentional noise, an evaluation accuracy around 90% is expected, robust, and healthy, preventing the tree from memorizing unrealistic rules.
